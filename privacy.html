SocialFlow Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

SocialFlow Studio is a personal tool used by a single owner to manage and schedule short videos on the owner's own TikTok and Facebook accounts.

Information we access. When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives basic profile information and an access token through TikTok Login Kit, and uses it only to publish videos the owner has chosen to post.

How information is stored. All data stays on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are encrypted locally. We do not store passwords, and we do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Retention and deletion. The owner can disconnect an account at any time in the app, which deletes its stored tokens. The owner can also revoke access from TikTok's settings.
